Method

The monitor takes the latest seven completed annual free-cash-flow-per-share values from MSN Money, averages them, converts the result from USD to AUD, grows that starting FCF/share for 10 years using Yahoo Finance's current earnings growth rate, discounts each year's FCF at 15%, and adds a terminal value equal to year-10 FCF/share × the GuruFocus 10-year median P/E.

Email alert: sent only when FMG crosses from above intrinsic value to below intrinsic value. It will not repeatedly email every day while the stock remains below the threshold.

This is a mechanical valuation monitor, not financial advice. A high or negative short-term earnings-growth figure can make a 10-year DCF unstable, so review the displayed growth rate before relying on the result.
