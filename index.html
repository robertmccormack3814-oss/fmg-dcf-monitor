Method

For each of the latest seven completed financial years, the monitor calculates free cash flow per share as total free cash flow divided by that year's shares outstanding. It then averages those seven per-share values, converts the average from USD to AUD, grows it for 10 years using Yahoo Finance's current earnings growth rate, discounts each year's FCF at 15%, and adds a terminal value equal to year-10 FCF/share × the GuruFocus 10-year median P/E.
